Our Story

A legacy of craftsmanship, trust, and timeless beauty since 2010

Our Journey

Built on Trust & Dedication

Fahad Jeweller was founded in 2010 by Gul Nawaz Khan in the heart of Nowshera. What began as a modest shop at Piple Chowk has grown into one of the most trusted jewellery destinations in the region, serving families across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa with honesty and excellence.

Gul Nawaz Khan's philosophy was simple: treat every customer like family, and never compromise on purity or quality. This commitment to integrity quickly earned the trust of the local community, and word spread throughout Saddar Nowshera and beyond.

Today, Fahad Jeweller stands as a testament to hard work and dedication. We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern designs, creating pieces that honour both our heritage and the evolving tastes of our valued customers across Pakistan.

🎯
